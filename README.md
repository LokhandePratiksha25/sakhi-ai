🌸 Sakhi AI – Maharashtra Women Welfare Scheme Assistant

📌 Overview

Sakhi AI is a women-centric welfare scheme assistant designed to help women in Maharashtra easily discover, understand, and access government welfare schemes relevant to different stages of life. The platform provides information about schemes related to education, child welfare, adolescent girls, employment, maternity, family support, and senior citizens through an easy-to-use chatbot interface.

The project aims to bridge the information gap between government welfare programs and beneficiaries by providing a centralized, user-friendly, and accessible platform.

---

🎯 Problem Statement

Many women, especially those from rural and underprivileged backgrounds, are unaware of the welfare schemes available to them. Information is often scattered across multiple websites, documents, and government portals, making it difficult to identify suitable schemes and understand eligibility criteria.

Sakhi AI addresses this challenge by providing a single platform where users can:

- Search government schemes easily
- Check eligibility criteria
- Explore schemes by category
- Access information in a user-friendly manner
- Use voice input instead of typing

---

✨ Features

🔍 Scheme Search

Search and explore Maharashtra Government women-centric welfare schemes.

📋 Eligibility Assistance

Helps users understand whether they qualify for a particular scheme based on eligibility requirements.

🏷️ Category-wise Exploration

Browse schemes based on different life stages and categories.

🎤 Voice-Based Search

Users can search for schemes using microphone input instead of typing.

This feature is particularly beneficial for:

- Rural women
- Semi-literate users
- Users unfamiliar with typing
- Women with limited digital literacy

🌐 Marathi Language Support

Supports Marathi terminology to improve accessibility and understanding.

💬 Chatbot Interface

Provides a conversational and user-friendly experience for scheme discovery.

📝 Feedback Collection System

Allows users to submit feedback and suggestions for continuous improvement.

🗄️ Database-Driven Architecture

Uses SQLite database for efficient scheme storage and retrieval.

☁️ Cloud Deployment

Successfully deployed on Render for public access.

🌟 Social Impact

Sakhi AI is designed with inclusivity and accessibility in mind.

Many women face challenges accessing welfare information due to:

- Language barriers
- Lack of digital literacy
- Difficulty navigating government websites
- Limited awareness of available schemes

The voice-enabled search feature and simplified interface help make welfare information accessible to a wider audience, especially women from rural and underserved communities.

📈 Current Scope

For the current project implementation, Sakhi AI includes information for approximately 50 Maharashtra Government welfare schemes related to women and girls.

These schemes have been carefully selected to demonstrate the functionality and impact of the platform.

---

⚠️ Current Limitations

- Currently contains only around 50 schemes.
- Database updates are performed manually.
- Does not yet provide direct application submission.
- Limited to Maharashtra women-centric welfare schemes.
- Voice search accuracy may depend on browser support and microphone quality.
- Does not currently integrate with live government databases.
